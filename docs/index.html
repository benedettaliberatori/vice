Video-Language Entailment for Video Similarity Estimation

Overview of ViCE: Given a video pair \((V_1, V_2)\) and a concept \(C_i\), ViCE processes the videos through two parallel branches. Each branch first generates a concept-conditioned caption for each video \((g_1, g_2)\), then performs video–caption entailment by comparing one video with the caption generated from its counterpart. The branches produce reasoning traces inside <thinking>...</thinking> and similarity scores inside <score>...</score>. The final similarity estimate \(s(V_1,V_2\mid C_i)\) is computed as the average of the scores generated from both branches.
